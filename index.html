WHO WE ARE

About Us

History

Established in 2026 at Kabi Nazrul Govt. College, Dhaka-1100, with a focus on academic and co-curricular activities in geography and environmental studies.

Objectives

Promote geographical knowledge, fieldwork, research, academic collaboration and professional skills among students and members.

Vision & Mission

Build a collaborative community that connects classroom learning with real-world geographical and environmental challenges.
